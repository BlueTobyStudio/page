Sonoklo Modding Policy

June 15th, 2026

As of the Effective Date, the Blue Toby Studio does not allow modding for the game Sonoklo. Sonoklo is currently in its early stage, and we prefer to stabilize and write the Sonoklo Design Guidelines correctly before allowing anyone to mod Sonoklo to prevent abuse. We believe that modding is a wonderful part of the community because creativity is the most wonderful gift of mankind.


THIS DECISION IS TEMPORARY AND THIS POLICY WILL BE UPDATED IN DUE TIME.

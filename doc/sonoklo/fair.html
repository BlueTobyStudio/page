Sonoklo Fair Play Policy

June 15th, 2026

This policy concerns all players of Sonoklo, on all platforms. While playing Sonoklo, doing the following is forbidden:

  • For all communications in or outside of Sonoklo with other players, the use of inappropriate language. This includes, but is not limited to:
    • Hate speech
    • Racism, misogyny, or any form of discrimination
    • Obscene, sexual, or gore content
    • Threats
    • Harassment
    • Any language aiming at hurting other people, Blue Toby Studio, or Sonoklo
  • Cheating with multiplayer features (such as Leaderboards and Achievements). Cheating is defined as using third-party software or exploiting unintended bugs to alter the game and gain an unfair advantage.
  • Selling, trading, or giving away your Sonoklo account or copy is prohibited.
  • Refund abuse.
  • Impersonation.
  • Exploiting multiplayer bugs.
  • Advertising without the Blue Toby Studio's consent.

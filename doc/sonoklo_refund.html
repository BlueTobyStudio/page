While playing Sonoklo on Android, you might encounter a popup with the title "Item refunded."

This popup is used to notify you that your entitlement to an in-app purchase has been revoked. This can only happen in two scenarios:

1. Your Google Play Store account has changed. If the new one no longer possesses the in-app purchase, the game automatically revokes the entitlement.

2. You asked for a refund for that in-app purchase.

If you believe that this action was a mistake, contact us at talk@bluetobystudio.com with the email address of your Google Play Store account and the purchase token of the involved purchase.
